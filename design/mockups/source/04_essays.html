I have always been passionate about robotics.1 In ninth grade I joined our school's robotics team, and by junior year I was the build lead. But the moment I'm proudest of didn't happen at a competition.

Last spring, our librarian asked if the team could run a STEM night for elementary students. I said yes before I knew what I was signing up for. The first night, 30 kids came,2 and we had four robots and one extension cord.

We kept going every other Thursday. I learned how to explain a motor controller to a nine-year-old, which is harder than building one. By May, some of the kids were building their own sensors and teaching their parents how they worked.3
