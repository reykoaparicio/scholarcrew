## Plan 20: browser extension

Overall: this is a careful plan.
- **Manifest.** MV3 asks for exactly `storage`, `activeTab`, and `scripting`. It has no `host_permissions`, `content_scripts`, `web_accessible_resources`, or `externally_connectable`, and a test pins all of this. The content script is injected only after the popup click, which is what grants `activeTab`.
- **Auth and CORS.** The token is checked against the same `sct_` + base64url shape that 06 issues (06 F-test `^sct_[A-Za-z0-9_-]{43}$`, 13 `^sct_[A-Za-z0-9_-]{8,200}$`). It's stored only in `chrome.storage.local` and is never echoed by the popup. It's sent only as `Authorization: Bearer` with `credentials: "omit"`, from the service worker. 13/14 already fulfil the plan's contract note (1): 14's two routes use `route({ auth: "bearer", cors: "extension" })` and `OPTIONS = extensionPreflight` (D11/D16). The extension's `Authorization`/`Content-Type` headers and GET/POST methods match 13's `corsHeaders`, and bearer auth skips the Origin check.
- **Scopes.** 13 `ApiTokenInput` defaults to `["autofill", "save_page"]`, and 16 posts only `{ label }`, so every token has both scopes. `requireScope` 403s are surfaced through `ErrorBody` mapping.
- **No-submit rule.** Rule 2 is enforced three ways: spies on `submit`/`requestSubmit`/`click`/`fetch`/`XMLHttpRequest`/`sendBeacon`, a source scan, and the gate grep. The panel uses a closed shadow root, `textContent`, and `type="button"`. `fillField` won't overwrite a filled field without `replace`.
- **Save page.** It sends exactly `url`, `title`, and `selectedText`, validated with `SavePageInput` (title ≤ 200, selection ≤ 2000, http(s) only).
- **Domain.** The extension imports `AutofillProfile`, `SavePageInput`, `ScholarshipSummary`, `Activity`, `ErrorBody`, and `ErrorCode` from `@scholarcrew/domain` and redefines none of them.
- **Tooling.** It uses only §2 packages (esbuild ^0.25.0, @types/chrome ^0.0.326, jsdom ^26.1.0, vitest ^3.2.4, tsx, typescript, zod ^4.0.0). Base `lib` includes `DOM.Iterable`, so the `for…of` loops over DOM collections typecheck.
- **Counts.** I traced the test counts (4 + 20 + 9 + 30 + 12 + 9 + 8 + 15 + 12 = 119 in 9 files) and they hold.
- **Rest.** The gates are commands with expected output, and there are no placeholders.
